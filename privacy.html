Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Summary: RetroBlasts is a free gaming website. We collect minimal data, do not sell your information, and prioritize your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on RetroBlasts, we collect:

  • Username: A display name you choose (not your real name)
  • Password: Stored securely using encryption
  • Game Data: Your scores, achievements, and in-game progress

We do NOT collect:

  • Real names or personal identification
  • Email addresses (unless you provide one for account recovery)
  • Payment information (the site is free)
  • Location data

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Saving your game progress and achievements
  • Displaying your username on leaderboards
  • Enabling multiplayer features

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We retain your data only as long as your account is active.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use:

  • Ko-fi: For voluntary donations (their privacy policy applies)
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (if enabled)

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies only:

  • Session cookies to keep you logged in
  • Preference cookies to remember your settings

6. Childrens Privacy

RetroBlasts is designed for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Accounts only require a username and password - no personal information.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account at any time from Settings
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us through our Ko-fi page.
